Privacy Policy - Behind the Case Publisher

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Behind the Case Publisher ("the app") is a private tool operated by one person, the owner of the Behind the Case accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. It is used only to publish the owner's own original short videos to the owner's own accounts. For TikTok, the owner reviews each video and approves the post before it is sent.

What the app accesses

  • It connects only to the owner's own accounts, after the owner logs in and approves access through each platform's official login.
  • On TikTok it reads the owner's basic profile (display name) and posting options, and it uploads and publishes the owner's own videos through the Content Posting API.
  • On Instagram it publishes the owner's own Reels. On YouTube it uploads the owner's own videos (youtube.upload scope only).

What the app stores

  • Access tokens for the owner's own accounts, kept only on the owner's computer.
  • A local log of which videos were published and when.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, read, store, sell or share data about any other user, follower, commenter or viewer.
  • It does not show platform data to anyone and has no other users.
  • It does not use platform data for advertising, profiling, or machine learning.

Sharing

The app does not share information with third parties. Videos are sent only to the platform they are published on.

Deleting data and revoking access

The owner can disconnect the app at any time in each platform's settings and by deleting the stored tokens from the owner's computer. To ask about data, email paladindevs@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.

Contact: paladindevs@gmail.com
